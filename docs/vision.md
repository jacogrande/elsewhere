# Elsewhere Vision

> An open-source RSS reader built for discovery. Let's use AI to find good writing.

## The Problem

1. I don't want to give $7 a month to Feedly. That's just silly.
2. I don't want to only see feeds by recency. All of these sources have years of high quality, interesting posts that, in a timeline feed, get lost in the ether. 
3. I don't want to go and find all my RSS sources myself.
4. I can't find new content/writers based on domains or knowledge.

## The Product
A free, open source, AI powered RSS reader that is built around a knowledge graph instead of a timeline.

**Core Features**
1. Source Discovery. Describe preferences and interests, and agents will find relevant blogs, feeds, publications, etc. As your interests evolve, the tool will build a source graph to find related writers based on what you're reading.
2. Knowledge Graph. We'll index, tag, and maybe even embed the knowledge within all of the articles you read. Then, we can build out knowledge graphs based on the topics you've become interested in, and you'll be able to explore new pieces based on that graph. For example, if you like the author's position on ux design hierarchy theory, you can view that in the graph, and our assistant will find related sources with similar, contrasting, and tangential knowledge.
3. Of course, you can still do simple timeline feed work.

## Conclusion
Let's make the small web easier to explore.

> Follow ideas, not time, not algorithms.
